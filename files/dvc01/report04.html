I need to stop describing control as if it’s stable. It isn’t. Every model I’ve tried that assumes a single controlling force fails under observation. At first, the system presents control as simple: Input → Action You press a direction. The character moves. You choose an option. The result follows. That’s how it’s supposed to work. But there are too many moments where that chain breaks— not visibly, but structurally. The outcome still happens. But the feeling of control doesn’t match the input.
OBSERVATION: RESISTANCE
There are instances where actions feel delayed— not in time, but in intent. It’s subtle: movement feels hesitant decisions feel weighted certain choices feel “wrong” in a way that isn’t mechanical This is usually dismissed as narrative tone. I don’t think that explanation holds anymore. Because resistance becomes explicit.
