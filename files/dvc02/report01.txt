(Foundational Technical Entry)

INITIAL OBSERVATIONS

- immediate response to external input
- directional synchronization

The final property is the most important.

If the SOUL can be removed while the host remains active,
then the SOUL cannot represent:
life
consciousness
or complete identity.

Instead, the evidence supports a different conclusion:

SOUL = CONTROL INTERFACE

Not the entity itself.
